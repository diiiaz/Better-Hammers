🔨 Mine multiple blocks at once with hammers !

## Features:
> **Excavation**: Swing your hammer to break multiple connected blocks in a single hit! Perfect for clearing out large areas or finding ores quickly. Be warned: this powerful action consumes extra durability and hunger.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/tGJ5D5v.gif" width="600" class="center" alt="Hammer mining a 3x3 tunnel."/><p>

> **Supercharge with New Enchantments!**: Take the destruction to the next level with two brand-new enchantments! Make your mining area wider or deeper than ever before.
>
> They’re fully compatible with all vanilla enchantments, so you can make your ultimate, unbreaking, fortune-bearing super-hammer!
> 
> _For detailed info on the new enchantments, pair it with the [Enchantment description](https://modrinth.com/project/UVtY3ZAC)_.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/4qN96WF.gif" width="600" class="center" alt="Hammer being enchanted with tunneler on an enchanting table."/><p>

> **Vanilla integration**: Better Hammers is perfectly woven into the vanilla experience. Hunt for hammers in ancient loot chests or wrestle them from the cold, dead hands of zombies!
